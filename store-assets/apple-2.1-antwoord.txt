Q1: Are there features in the app that are not represented in the

    friends list.

A2: YES - and all of them are listed here in full. Each is contacted in
    the recording.

    1. https://ldzdfgfaqiwwdogpltsu.supabase.co  (Supabase, EU region)
       Our own backend. Used for account sign-in (Sign in with Apple,

Q3: Can functionality or features be changed or introduced to the app
    after App Review approval without submitting a new binary?

A3: NO. The app is a self-contained HTML5/JavaScript game wrapped with
    Capacitor. All game code, assets, levels, heroes and maps are
    included in the binary and are loaded from the local app bundle.
    The app does not load, download, or execute any code from a remote
    source, and there is no remote configuration, feature flag, or
    A/B mechanism that can alter its behaviour. Our backend is used
    only for the player's own account data and for synchronising online
    matches, as described in question 2. Any change to the game itself
    requires a new binary submitted to App Review.

Q4: Does the app process, transmit, or store payment, financial,
    health, medical, biometric, or other sensitive data that is not
    declared in the App Privacy questions?

A4: NO. All purchases are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit 2; the
    app never sees or stores any payment or financial data. The app
    collects no health, medical, biometric, or location data. The only
    personal data we store is the user's e-mail address and self-chosen
    nickname for their account, plus their game progress - exactly as
    declared in our App Privacy answers. Users can permanently delete
    their account and all associated data from within the app
    (Settings > Account > Delete account), shown in the recording.

Q5: Does the app reference, integrate with, or share branding, naming,
    or visuals with any third-party brand, hardware product, financial
    institution, or other protected service?

A5: YES, limited to two standard sign-in integrations, both used as an
    authentication option on our login screen:

    - "Sign in with Apple", implemented and presented in accordance with
      Apple's Human Interface Guidelines for Sign in with Apple.
    - "Continue with Google" (Google Sign-In), implemented and presented
      in accordance with Google's Sign-In branding guidelines.

    Both are shown in the recording. Apart from these, the app contains
    no third-party brands, logos, or visuals - all artwork, characters,
    music and sound effects are original work created by us.
