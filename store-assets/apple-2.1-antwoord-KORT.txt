Our answers to your five questions:

1. Features not represented in the screenshots, description or
category? NO. Every feature is covered: online 1v1 matches, matches
against bots, the singleplayer Journey campaign, unlockable heroes,
cosmetic skins, the Blacksmith (forging armour), the shop, and the
leaderboard and friends list.

2. Domains, URLs or external services? YES - all listed here, and all
contacted in the recording:
- https://ldzdfgfaqiwwdogpltsu.supabase.co (Supabase, EU region): our
  own backend, used for account sign-in (Apple, Google, e-mail), cloud
  storage of the player's game progress, the leaderboard and friends
  list, and the realtime WebSocket that synchronises online 1v1
  matches.
- https://rymrheroes.thebrandingfive.nl: our own website, opened in an
  in-app browser only when the user taps Privacy Policy, Support, or
  "Share an idea".
- https://formsubmit.co: form-mail service used only by the "Share an
  idea" page on that website (not by the app itself).
- https://apps.apple.com: opened only when the user taps "Write a
  review", linking to our own App Store product page.

3. Can functionality change after approval without a new binary? NO.
The app is a self-contained HTML5/JavaScript game wrapped with
Capacitor. All code, assets, levels, heroes and maps are in the binary
and loaded from the local app bundle. The app does not load, download
or execute any remote code, and there is no remote configuration or
feature-flag mechanism that can alter its behaviour. Our backend is
used only for the user's own account data and online match
synchronisation. Any change to the game requires a new binary.

4. Payment, financial, health, medical, biometric or other sensitive
data not declared? NO. All purchases are handled entirely by Apple's
StoreKit 2; the app never sees payment or financial data. No health,
medical, biometric or location data is collected. We store only the
user's e-mail address, self-chosen nickname and game progress, exactly
as declared in our App Privacy answers. Users can permanently delete
their account and all data in the app (Settings > Account > Delete
account).

5. Third-party branding? YES, limited to two standard sign-in options
on our login screen: "Sign in with Apple" (per Apple's Human Interface
Guidelines) and "Continue with Google" (per Google's Sign-In branding
guidelines). Both appear in the recording. All other artwork,
characters, music and sound effects are original work created by us.

Additional information: third-party libraries are Capacitor 6, the
supabase-js client for our own backend, and Apple's StoreKit 2 - all
bundled in the binary. There are no hidden or dormant features. The
only content other players can see is a self-chosen nickname on the
leaderboard and friends list; free-text chat is disabled in this
version. Crates with randomised contents disclose their exact odds in
the app before purchase (guideline 3.1.1).
