Frequently asked questions

  • I lost my progress. Progress is saved to your account. Log in with the same account (Sign in with Apple, Google or e-mail) and your heroes, coins and Journey progress come back automatically.
  • I bought rubies but did not receive them. Fully close and reopen the app — purchases are restored on launch. Still missing? Mail us your Apple receipt and we will sort it out.
  • The online match disconnected. Matches that lose connection are cancelled without affecting your rank or coins. Playing on Wi-Fi gives the most stable experience.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app: menu → settings icon → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all progress.
  • Is there sound on silent mode? Yes, game audio plays even with the ring switch on silent. You can turn music and sound effects off in the settings.
